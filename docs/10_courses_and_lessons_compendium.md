## 2. Flagship Standalone YouTube Masterclasses & Specialist Creators

### End-to-End Anime Production & Industry Talks
- **Noggi — *EVERYTHING About Creating Beautiful 3D Anime* (`xZWxEoa-Dwk`, 15+ hrs, 42 chapters)**: Covers NPR setup (`24fps`, `Standard`, Motion Blur OFF), Sculpt vs Poly modeling, Light Linking/Shadow tricks, Custom Normals, Rigging, Limited Animation (`2s`/`3s`/mixed rates), Grease Pencil, Line Art, VFX, and Compositing. Also *I Studied How Guilty Gear Makes 3D Look Perfectly 2D* (`tWcaQ3gCbUU`).
- **Arc System Works / GDC 2015 — Junya C Motomura (*GuiltyGearXrd's Art Style: The X Factor Between 2D and 3D*, `yhGjCzxJV3E`)** + Japanese technical lecture (`4KOoN65ZcrI`): Limited frame-skipping, custom directional shadows, vertex-color line thickness, square UVs, and camera-view distortion cheats.
- **SunnyIsOnline ft. Dillongoo (`4LhvcINgNxU`)**: How Dillongoo Studio creates fast-paced 3D anime fight scenes in Blender (`Stepped timing`, squash/stretch, silhouettes, compositing).
- **Natsumori Katsu (`Sy7RHtco43Y`, `Ng8FbOHvsLQ`)**: Complete indie VTuber/VRChat pipeline—poly modeling anime body, low-res Beveled Hair Curve ribbons, 3 Rigify rules (`X=0` center spine, unbroken parented chains, elbow/knee slight pre-bend), and limited animation tips.

### Anime Faces, Eyes, Hair, Clothes & Retopology
- **Shonzo (`HHCGrbeTWww`, `w064sEhntpc`)**: Proportions of modern 3D anime heads (38%–45% eye line, short nose/chin triangle), side profile shadow planes, and stylized hair flow.
- **2AM (`NCIOXxjw8BQ`, `hZMO3OgV-Lw`, `uptuXWNWpk0`, `x-K6bCAl6Qs`, `LU0BFThJIco`, `3guG5h5jX-E`, `blgGcbZzsWM`, `Qyrz4JoMP28`)**: Dedicated specialist series covering stylized 3D anime eyes (curved iris dish, lash separation), tilted-plane face normal editing (`oKfFi1MpO9c`), 7-step **SDF Face Shadow node math**, hair modeling/texturing/normals, **`Link 2` skirt rigging + bust/hair springs**, and **VRM lip-sync & blinking export**.
- **Chaosy3D (`yg8Snn3OzLc`)**, **Bran Sculpts (`0SRq5i_v83c`, `J2_uiUEcY7w`)**, **Silver William (`fNxdOtegry0`)**, **FlyCat (`F_RgaCzgMwQ`)**: High-speed stylization & sculpting workflows (Kill la Kill / Ryuko anime sculpts, poly female base meshes).
- **Maoly Studio (`QVwzmYTNMZc`, `rvDf6ZuEynM`, `5DaLb-d5Ies`, `aKpIM5DZaFc`)**, **Vertex Arcade (`0aPr51sDkxE`)**, **Kenny Praise (`ilzlcTb4GTk`)**: Clean stylized face modeling/sculpting and manual retopology (`Shrinkwrap`, `F2`, `Slide Relax`, ear extraction).
- **Astonished Animations (`HGKYUWI44lQ`)**, **PyroDev (`gOvm7XuI9Qo`)**, **Facepalme Dev (`ATClZK6W5a8`)**, **Riel (`46F7k4fvsN8`)**, **Chicha (`jYgiT35wES0`)**: Gacha-style minimalist hair topology (`3-edge cross-section`), `Ucupaint` free layered painting, UDIM-to-atlas concepts, and UV Vectorizing (`Follow Active Quads` + straight border cuts).

### Normals, Rigging Physics, Animation & Blender 5.x Workflows
- **Activemotionpictures / 3dcinetv (`79XKQVAS9dI`, `aIA7tkd-M5o`)**, **AnimeXD (`KB0B9Hz46UM`, `1puHJSkZy24`)**, **Fondant (`9JiewKzta7Y`)**, **Blender Secrets (`dybTdt9WXts`, `QGV3v0wom1E`)**: Normal editing facial zones, keeping an undeformed `Data Transfer` head copy, `Laplacian` normal smoothing + Inverted Area Weighting, and Blender 5.2 Cycles/EEVEE `Inverted Hull` (`Backfacing` + `Is Camera Ray` / `Ray Length`).
- **TomCAT (`IOjH0eG43Ak`, 3.5 hrs)**: Rigify long hair chains, attaching eyes/teeth/tongue, and multi-layer weight cleanup.
- **Pierrick Picaut (Art of Effective Rigging)**: Weight painting secrets (`Auto Weights`, clean joint bends), skirt & dynamic cloth rigging, and bone-driven deformers.
